About Us

About DD Prime Solution

DD Prime Solution works with startups, shop owners, freelancers and small business owners across India who need reliable support for two things that usually pull them in opposite directions: building a digital presence, and getting business paperwork in order.

We bring both under one roof — website design and development, digital marketing, social media management and branding on one side, and documentation assistance for GST, MSME/Udyam and FSSAI applications, along with general business registration assistance, on the other.

We are a documentation and digital support service — not a law firm, chartered accountancy, or government body. We help you prepare, organise and submit the right information through the correct official channels, so you can focus on running your business.
